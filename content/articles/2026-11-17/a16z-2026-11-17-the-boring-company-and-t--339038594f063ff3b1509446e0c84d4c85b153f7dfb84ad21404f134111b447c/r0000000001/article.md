## Bilingual reading

### s1 (paragraph, order 0)

The authors profile a tunneling company that is using a working local transport service as a starting point for more ambitious projects. The Las Vegas operation provides an operating example; the intercity promises remain a separate question.

作者以一家隧道公司为例，说明如何从已经投入运营的短程交通服务出发，尝试更大规模的项目。拉斯维加斯提供了运营案例，但不能替尚未落地的城际设想背书。

### s2 (paragraph, order 1)

A faster cutting head is not necessarily the quickest route to a completed tunnel. The profile credits changes to the sequence of work, equipment setup and routine stoppages. Taken together, those changes increase the share of a workday spent making progress.

更快的刀盘不一定就能更快完成隧道。文章把改进归因于施工顺序、设备进场和日常停工环节的调整。合在一起，这些变化提高了一个工作日内有效推进所占的比例。

### s3 (paragraph, order 2)

The Nashville example illustrates why infrastructure teams cannot assume that a design for one city transfers unchanged to another. Different ground conditions can alter the equipment, tasks and economics.

纳什维尔的案例说明，为一座城市设计的方案不能原样照搬到另一座城市。地质条件不同，设备、工序和经济账都会跟着变化。

### s4 (paragraph, order 3)

The article sees a potential link between cheaper access to employment centers and more usable residential land. That possibility depends on where stations are located and who can use them, not merely on whether another tunnel can be excavated.

文章把更便宜的交通接入与更多可居住土地联系起来。能否兑现这层价值取决于站点位置和哪些人能用到服务，而不仅仅是能不能再挖一条隧道。

## Analysis

文章实际提供了两个有不同证据等级的判断：降低隧道施工成本在局部项目中已有观察，高速城际网络及住房可负担性变化仍是待验证的外推。 / The evidence for local construction improvement should not be treated as proof of an intercity network or lower housing costs.
- 文章以已运营的 Vegas Loop 说明短线产品与概念图的区别，同时用多项工序改进展示完工周期并不只由瞬时钻进速度决定。 / The existing Loop is a different kind of evidence from the construction process examples or long-term forecasts.
- 文章对比内华达与纳什维尔的施工条件。这个对照更支持按地质条件选型，而不是把某个项目的单价普遍化。 / Differences in geology limit how broadly a unit cost can be applied.

### discussion

- 项目采购可以同时要求每隧道英里、每线路英里和包含站点等费用的总预算。三者回答不同的成本问题。 / Compare tunnel-mile, route-mile and whole-project costs rather than treating them as interchangeable.
- 交通方案要以峰时实际运送人数、等待和故障恢复为验收标准。乘客可达范围扩大与否应再单独验证。 / Peak throughput, waits and recovery matter independently of construction speed.
- 优先选择一条需求清楚的短走廊，事先锁定预算范围、峰时运力和可靠性目标。只有在完整线路达到这些指标后，才把结果用于评估复制；先不把城际速度或房租变化纳入基准。 / Evaluate one corridor against predefined whole-project cost, capacity and reliability before extrapolating.

### limitations

作者披露 a16z 参与了融资，本文是投资方发布的公司特写，不是独立工程审计。 / The publisher discloses its investment; company-reported results are not independent engineering audits.

站点、换乘、无障碍与长期运维成本会改变方案比较。文中工程局部指标不能直接推出总成本或租金变化。 / Access, interchange and recurring costs may change the comparison; the article does not identify a causal rental effect.

### independent judgment

优先选择一条需求清楚的短走廊，事先锁定预算范围、峰时运力和可靠性目标。只有在完整线路达到这些指标后，才把结果用于评估复制；先不把城际速度或房租变化纳入基准。 / Evaluate one corridor against predefined whole-project cost, capacity and reliability before extrapolating.
