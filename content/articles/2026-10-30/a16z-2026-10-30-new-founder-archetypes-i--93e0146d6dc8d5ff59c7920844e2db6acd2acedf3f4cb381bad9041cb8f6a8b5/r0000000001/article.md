## Analysis

原文核心观点 / Source thesis: AI should prompt a reassessment of founder stereotypes, not another fixed template. AI 应推动重新审视创业者刻板印象，而不是再造一套固定模板。
- 来源定位 / Source locator: https://a16z.com/new-founder-archetypes-in-the-ai-age/，作者署名与页面发布日期已核实；本文是观点文章，不把其中的能力倍数描述当作测量结果。

### discussion

- 独立推演 / Independent inference: A practical evaluation can separate delivery, demand and recovery. Delivery asks whether one real workflow finishes; demand asks whether someone returns without prompting; recovery asks whether failures can be diagnosed and reversed. These are proposed review dimensions, not findings measured by the source. 实务评估可以拆成三个维度：交付看真实流程能否完成，需求看用户是否无需催促就再次使用，恢复看失败能否定位并回滚。这是本文提出的评审维度，不是原文测得的结论。
- 独立推演 / Independent inference: Hiring should target the current failure mode. If tests expose data loss, add engineering review; if nobody repeats the workflow, investigate the problem and audience before increasing output. More shipped features are not a substitute for evidence about the weak link. 招人应针对当前失败模式：若测试暴露数据丢失，应增加工程审查；若无人重复使用流程，应先调查问题与人群，而不是增加产出。多发布功能不能替代对薄弱环节的验证。
- 原创判断 / Independent judgment: Run a bounded two-week experiment around one recurring job. Record the user, completion condition, manual rescue time and failure cost before building. At the end, inspect completed jobs and repeat use, then choose among continuing alone, adding a complementary partner or narrowing the scope. Keep an explicit stop condition so enthusiasm cannot silently extend the trial. This is a proposed decision process, not evidence that any founder category performs better. 围绕一个重复发生的任务做有边界的两周实验。开发前记录用户、完成条件、人工救援时间与失败成本。结束时检查实际完成量和重复使用，再决定继续单人推进、引入互补伙伴或缩小范围。预先写明停止条件，避免热情悄悄延长试验。这是建议的决策流程，不证明哪类创业者更优秀。

### limitations

阅读边界 / Reading boundary: This archive is an original summary, not a translation of the complete article. 本归档是原创摘要，不是原文全文翻译。

独立方法限制 / Independent methodological limit: Comparisons between teams need the same task, time window and quality threshold. Otherwise a polished demo, a working service and a paid deployment may be counted as equivalent output when they are not. 比较团队需要相同任务、时间窗口和质量门槛，否则精美演示、可用服务与付费部署会被错误地算成同等产出。

独立方法限制 / Independent methodological limit: A short trial cannot establish long-term retention or organizational resilience. Proposed metrics require longitudinal observation and should not become a new universal founder score. 短期试验不能确立长期留存或组织韧性。建议指标仍需长期观察，也不应变成新的通用创业者评分。
